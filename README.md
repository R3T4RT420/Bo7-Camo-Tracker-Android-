BO7 Camo Tracker

The BO7 Camo Tracker is a personal project I made to make tracking weapon camo challenges in Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 easier. It helps you keep track of your camo progress without having to remember every challenge or check everything in-game.

Info: At the moment only german language because im german and this is the first update.

How to Use

1. Open the Tracker
-Open the tracker using the link provided below.
-Tracker Link: https://r3t4rt420.github.io/Bo7-Camo-Tracker-Android-/

2. Select a Weapon
Choose the weapon you are currently working on and want to track your progress for.

3. Track Your Camo Progress
Check the camo challenges for your selected weapon and mark the ones you have already completed. This makes it easier to see which challenges are finished and which ones you still need to complete.

4. Keep Track of Your Progress
Update your progress as you play and use the tracker to keep your camo grind organized.

Android Installation:
You can install the tracker on your Android device and use it like a regular app.

1. Open the tracker link in Google Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
3. Select Install app or Add to Home screen.
4. Confirm the installation.

The tracker will then appear on your home screen, allowing you to open it quickly without having to enter the link every time.
